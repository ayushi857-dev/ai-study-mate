# 🎓 AI StudyMate

> **Your Personal AI-Powered Learning Assistant**

AI StudyMate is an intelligent learning platform designed to make studying more **personalized, interactive, and efficient**. It brings AI-powered tutoring, study planning, quiz generation, and learning history together in one focused learning experience.

## ✨ Features

- 🤖 **AI Tutor** — Get clear, step-by-step explanations for academic and programming concepts.
- 📚 **Study Planner** — Create structured and personalized study plans.
- 🧠 **Quiz Generator** — Generate practice quizzes to test and strengthen your knowledge.
- 📊 **Study History** — Track your learning activities and progress.
- 💬 **Interactive Learning** — Ask questions and learn through natural conversations.
- 👤 **Personalized Experience** — Get learning support tailored to your needs.

## 🧠 AI-Powered Learning

AI StudyMate uses **Google Gemini** to provide intelligent, conversational, and context-aware learning assistance.

The platform is designed to help students move beyond simply finding answers and focus on **understanding concepts, practicing effectively, and learning consistently**.

## 🎯 Our Goal

The goal of AI StudyMate is simple:

> **Make learning smarter, more accessible, and more engaging.**

By bringing tutoring, planning, practice, and learning history together, AI StudyMate creates a more focused and personalized study experience.

AI StudyMate can evolve with:
